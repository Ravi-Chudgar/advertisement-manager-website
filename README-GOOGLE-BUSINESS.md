## 📱 Publishing to Google Business Profile

### Step 1: Prepare Your Images

You'll need high-quality images of microwaves. Here are options:

**Option A: Use Stock Images**
- Visit free stock photo sites:
  - Unsplash.com (search "microwave repair")
  - Pexels.com (search "microwave oven")
  - Pixabay.com (search "kitchen appliance")

**Option B: Take Your Own Photos**
- Take clear photos of:
  - Different microwave types you repair
  - Your technician working
  - Before/after repair shots
  - Your service van or workshop

**Option C: Use the Emoji Placeholders**
- The HTML file includes emoji placeholders that work well for social media

### Step 2: Access Google Business Profile

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Select your business profile

### Step 3: Create a New Post

1. Click on **"Posts"** in the left menu
2. Click **"Create post"** or **"+"** button
3. Choose **"Offer"** post type

### Step 4: Fill in Post Details

Using the information from `microwave-ad-data.json`:

**Title:**
```
Expert Microwave Repair Services - Diagnosis Just ₹250
```

**Description:**
```
Professional microwave repair with complete diagnosis and root cause detection for just ₹250! If you proceed with repair, the diagnosis fee is fully deducted from your total bill.

✅ Expert Technicians
✅ Genuine Parts
✅ 30-Day Warranty
✅ Home Service Available

We service all major brands: Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, Panasonic, IFB, Godrej, Haier, Bosch

📍 Service Area: [Your City/Area]
📞 Call: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
💬 WhatsApp: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
```

**Offer Details:**
- **Offer Title**: Special Diagnosis Offer
- **Coupon Code**: MICRO250
- **Start Date**: [Today's Date]
- **End Date**: [Your Choice]
- **Terms**: 
  - Diagnosis fee of ₹250 applies to all microwave brands and models
  - Full deduction from repair cost if customer proceeds with repair
  - Valid for residential and commercial microwaves
  - Service available in [Your City/Area] and surrounding regions

**Call to Action:**
- **Button**: "Call Now"
- **URL**: `tel:+91XXXXXXXXXX`

### Step 5: Add Images

1. Click **"Add photo"** or **"Add media"**
2. Upload your microwave images
3. Use 1-3 high-quality images
4. Recommended size: 1200x1200 pixels (square)

### Step 6: Preview and Publish

1. Review your post preview
2. Check all details are correct
3. Click **"Publish"** or **"Post"**

## 🎨 Customization Guide

### Update Contact Information

Replace these placeholders in all files:

**Phone Numbers:**
- `+91 XXXXX XXXXX` → Your actual phone number
- `+91 XXXXX XXXXX` → Your WhatsApp number

**Email:**
- `your-email@example.com` → Your business email

**Address:**
- `[Your Complete Address]` → Your business address
- `[Your City/Area]` → Your service area

**Business Name:**
- `[Your Business Name]` → Your actual business name

### Update Pricing

If you want to change the diagnosis fee:

1. Update in `microwave-repair-ad.html`:
   - Line with `₹250`
   - Price section

2. Update in `microwave-ad-data.json`:
   - `offer.description` field
   - `offer.termsConditions` array

### Update Service Area

Change the service area text to match your actual coverage:
- Replace `[Your City/Area]` with your specific locations
- Add specific neighborhoods or regions you serve

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Post Not Showing:**
- Check if your business is verified
- Ensure post meets Google's guidelines
- Wait 24-48 hours for indexing

**Low Engagement:**
- Try different posting times
- Improve image quality
- Update your offer description
- Add more specific details

**No Calls:**
- Verify phone number is correct
- Check if call tracking is enabled
- Ensure your business hours are accurate
