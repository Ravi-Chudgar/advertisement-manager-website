## 🚀 Quick Start - 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Get Your Images
- Take photos of microwaves you repair
- Or download free stock images from Unsplash/Pexels
- Recommended: 1200x1200 pixels, square format

### Step 2: Customize the Content
Open `GOOGLE-BUSINESS-POST-CONTENT.txt` and replace:
- `+91 XXXXX XXXXX` → Your phone number
- `your-email@example.com` → Your email
- `[Your City/Area]` → Your service area
- `[Add Date]` → Your offer end date

### Step 3: Post to Google Business Profile
1. Go to business.google.com
2. Click "Posts" → "Create post"
3. Copy content from `GOOGLE-BUSINESS-POST-CONTENT.txt`
4. Upload your images
5. Click "Publish"
